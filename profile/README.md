# Probe

## The smart camera for every trades technician
**Tagline:** Every technician's phone becomes the smartest tool the company owns.

Probe is an AI camera workflow for plumbing, HVAC, and electrical field service teams.  
A technician snaps a photo of a broken part, and in under a minute Probe helps them:

- identify likely failure modes,
- pull live local parts availability and pricing,
- generate a clear, signable estimate for customer approval.

## Why this matters
Today, technicians usually diagnose verbally and type notes by hand. That slows jobs, creates pricing mistrust, and loses valuable field intelligence. Probe turns the on-site camera moment into a faster, more transparent quoting flow.

## What makes Probe different
- **Photo-first diagnosis:** built around images, not text-only inputs.
- **Live distributor pricing:** quotes reflect real local stock and pricing.
- **Outcome learning loop:** recommendations improve from real acceptance outcomes over time.
- **Compounding data advantage:** every field photo and result strengthens the model.
